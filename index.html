Brief Bio

I am currently a researcher at Ant Group, working on multimodal large language models. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU). I received my Ph.D. from Peking University in 2022 under the supervision of Prof. Shiliang Zhang. My current research interests include MLLM agents, multimodal reasoning, and related areas.
